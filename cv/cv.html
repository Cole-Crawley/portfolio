Profile

I'm a UX and web designer who has just finished an MSc in Computer Science at the University of Lincoln. I work out how a website or app should flow, design how it looks and reads, and test it against real use before calling it done. Because I can also build what I design, I spot a platform's limits early and nothing gets lost in a handoff. My work ranges from a live site for a local business to an AI study app I evaluated against usability heuristics. I pick up new tools quickly and like work with a clear point of view.
