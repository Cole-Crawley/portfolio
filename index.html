About

A 2000s kid from Manila, always out playing by 4pm.

I grew up in a condo in Mandaluyong: a white building with green lettering, birthdays by the pool on white plastic chairs. At my grandparents' place there was a sari-sari store on every corner, and my pocket money went on Choc Nut. The packets on this page come from there.

I'm a UX and UI designer who builds what he designs. Over an MSc and four projects, three of them live, I've taken products from first idea to launch, most recently finding and fixing 11 usability issues in a study app.
